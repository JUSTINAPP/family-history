# Replaceable demo assets

All source photographs and scans are used as visual illustrations, not identified family records. Fictional UI labels intentionally do not claim historical accuracy. The public `/about` route also explains this distinction. Do not infer a relationship, date, place or identity from fixture associations.

Images are served locally and optimised by Next.js for this public demo. Downloaded source assets remain unchanged; UI crops are presentational. Never put private family originals in this folder. Future originals and their derivatives belong to separate private Storage buckets with membership-gated delivery.

Fonts: DM Sans by Colophon Foundry / contributors, and Libre Caslon Display by Impallari Type / contributors, self-hosted from Google Fonts distributions under SIL Open Font License 1.1. Licence files are in `public/fonts`. Fonts are loaded locally; no Google Fonts service request happens at runtime.

## Voice-flow example

`public/audio/example-recollection.wav` is an approximately 8.6-second synthetic spoken example generated locally with the installed macOS Karen speech voice. The words are the fictional Janice/Shropshire recollection shown in the capture UI. It is explicitly labelled as a demo recording; it is not a real family member or historical testimony. No voice cloning, microphone capture or external speech service is used. Replace this fixture with real, consented contributor audio only after private original storage and auth are connected.
